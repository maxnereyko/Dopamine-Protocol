# Tokenomics

Dopamine Protocol uses a 3-token architecture designed for zero volatility and sustainable long-term growth.

## DOPE — Action & Prediction Token

- **Type:** ERC-20 (pegged 1:1 to USDC)
- **Function:** All action contract collateral, challenge stakes, liquidity pool deposits, and prize pool contributions.
- **Mechanism:** 1 DOPE = $1.00 USDC. Eliminates token volatility risk for participants.

## TIME — Service & Mentorship Token

- **Type:** ERC-20 (pegged 1:1 to USDC)
- **Function:** Medium of exchange within the Services Marketplace. Used to pay for coaching, mentorship, and validation.
- **Mechanism:** Stable transactional asset. Professionals set their own rates in TIME.

## MOTIV — Proof-of-Will Reputation Asset

- **Type:** ERC-5192 / Soulbound Token (non-transferable)
- **Function:** Unforgeable proof of discipline, governance voting power, and yield qualification.
- **Acquisition:** Cannot be purchased. Only minted by completing verified Action Contracts or accurately validating disputes.
- **Utility:** DAO voting rights, reduced protocol fees (down to 1%), staking rewards from protocol fees.

## Fee Structure

- **5%** protocol fee on all winning Prediction Pools and Talent Contest funds
- **2.5%** service fee on peer-to-peer mentorship transactions in TIME

## Revenue Redistribution

| Allocation | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| 50% | DAO Treasury — operations, liquidity, security audits |
| 30% | MOTIV Staker Yield — distributed in USDC |
| 10% | BEST Creator Fund — viral challenge rewards |
| 10% | Core Operations — infrastructure and expansion |
